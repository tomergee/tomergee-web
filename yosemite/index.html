Yosemite

Five landmarks over three nights at Evergreen Lodge — a car-light plan for six travelers driving up from Irvine, Aug 1–4.

01

The Sights & The Plan

From your base at Evergreen Lodge you'll enter through Big Oak Flat (Hwy 120), about 45 minutes from the valley floor. The five highlights split into two clusters — the valley itself and the Wawona corridor to the south — which is exactly how the days below are grouped.

The Five Sites

1

Tunnel View

Drive · no shuttleNo hikingSunset best

The postcard shot — El Capitan, Bridalveil Fall, and Half Dome in one frame. It's right on Hwy 41 as you drive in, so make it your arrival-evening stop. Small lots on both sides; come 45 min before sunset.

2

Yosemite Falls

ShuttleEasy → strenuous

Tallest waterfall in North America. The Lower Falls loop is a flat, 1-mile stroll anyone can do; the Upper Falls trail is a lung-busting 7.2-mile round trip. Ride the Valleywide shuttle to the Lower Yosemite Fall stop.

3

Glacier Point

Drive · ~1 hrSeasonalShort paved walk

A 3,200-ft aerial view over the valley and Half Dome. Drive up Glacier Point Rd (summer–fall only) or take the paid tour bus from Yosemite Valley Lodge. Best light is morning.

4

Mariposa Grove

Mandatory shuttleSeasonal

500+ giant sequoias, including the ~3,000-year-old Grizzly Giant. Park at the Welcome Plaza near the south entrance and ride the free grove shuttle. Pairs naturally with a south-end lodging base.

5

Mist Trail / Half Dome

Shuttle onlyStrenuousPermit for cables

Cars can't reach the Happy Isles trailhead — take the East Valley shuttle. The Mist Trail soaks you at Vernal Fall (~3 mi RT) or pushes on to Nevada Fall (~6 mi). Half Dome's cables need a lottery permit.

Your Days · Aug 1–4

Sat, Aug 1 · Drive up

Irvine → Evergreen Lodge

Leave by 6 AM — it's ~6½ hrs to Evergreen via Hwy 120 through Groveland, longer with summer traffic. Arrive mid-afternoon, settle in, keep the evening easy. (Big drive plus a Saturday valley = save the sights for the quieter days.)

Sun, Aug 2 · South corridor

Tunnel View → Glacier Point → Mariposa Grove

Take the drive-heavy south day on the busiest day, when you want to be out of the packed valley. All three line up along the Wawona corridor: Tunnel View for the classic overlook, up to Glacier Point, then the sequoias at Mariposa Grove. Budget real windshield time — it's ~1½ hrs each way from Evergreen.

Mon, Aug 3 · Valley floor

Yosemite Falls & the Mist Trail

Save the valley for Monday, when parking and shuttles ease up. Enter early via Big Oak Flat, park once at Yosemite Village, and shuttle between Yosemite Falls, El Cap Meadow and Bridalveil — then Curry Village to the East Valley shuttle for the Mist Trail.

Tue, Aug 4 · Drive home

Evergreen → Irvine

Pack and roll out by 8–9 AM to stay ahead of gate traffic, and you'll be home for dinner.

August heads-up: Yosemite Falls runs on snowmelt and is often low — or bone dry — by early August, so treat it as a viewpoint, not a gusher. The Mist Trail's Vernal & Nevada Falls (Merced River) hold up far better. It's also peak heat and peak crowds: start early, carry plenty of water.

Optional swap — that south day is a lot of driving from a north base. Evergreen sits close to Tioga Road, so if the group would rather cut windshield time, trade Glacier Point + Mariposa Grove for the high country: Olmsted Point, Tenaya Lake and Tuolumne Meadows are roughly an hour east and spectacular in August.

02

Getting Around

The park's own rule sets the whole strategy: if you find a parking space, leave your car there — you won't find another. Here's how to move six people through the valley without fighting for spots twice a day.

The two-car plan

Drive up in both cars — six people plus luggage need the space for a 6–7 hour haul. Once you reach Evergreen, park car #2 and leave it. Run each park day on one car + the free shuttle. You'll pay to enter both vehicles, but only wrestle one into a valley lot.

Your entrance · Big Oak Flat (Hwy 120)

From Evergreen you'll enter at Big Oak Flat, where gate delays run ~30 min on busy days — far better than the South entrance's 1–2 hours. It's about 45 min from the lodge to the valley floor. The Aug 2 south-corridor day is the exception: you'll drive through the valley and down Wawona Rd, making it your longest driving day. Whatever the day, roll out of Evergreen early — be at the gate before 8 AM — to skip the queue and actually find parking.

Live parking & traffic: text "ynptraffic" to 333111 before leaving Evergreen each morning.

The free shuttles

Two loops, both free, both running 7 AM–10 PM. The Valleywide shuttle hits every major stop every 12–22 min (a full loop is ~1.5 hrs). The East Valley shuttle is more frequent (every 8–12 min) and is the only way to reach Happy Isles / the Mist Trail and Mirror Lake.

Passes

Two vehicles means $35 × 2 = $70 in 7-day entrance passes (US residents pay no per-person surcharge). If anyone plans to visit another national park this year — Sequoia, Joshua Tree, Death Valley are all close — buy one America the Beautiful pass ($80) for one car instead; it covers that vehicle at every federal site for a year and is available as a digital pass in 2026.

Reminder: no entrance reservation is required in 2026 — but that's driving record crowds. Weekdays and early arrivals are your friend.
